Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Lightcord

Is Lightcord safe to use?

Yes! Lightcord modifies the Discord client locally on your machine. It does not share your data and is perfectly safe. However, modifying clients is technically against Discord's ToS, though bans for themes/plugins are virtually non-existent.

Can I use BetterDiscord themes and plugins?

Absolutely. Lightcord is fully compatible with the vast majority of BetterDiscord themes and plugins. You can just drag and drop them into your Lightcord folders.

Is Lightcord completely free?

Yes! Lightcord is a completely free project built for the community.

Does it consume a lot of RAM or CPU?

Not at all. Lightcord is heavily optimized to be lightweight and fast, meaning you get all the extra features without sacrificing performance or battery life.
