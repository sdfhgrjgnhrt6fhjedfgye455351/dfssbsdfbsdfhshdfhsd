Architecture

Engineered Capabilities

Lightcord bundles key enhancements directly into the client runtime without third-party bot tokens or complicated manual injections.

MusicBot

Lossless 48kHz stereo streaming directly into any voice channel. Features multi-track queuing, dynamic synchronized lyrics, volume normalization, and EQ boost without server bot invites.

BetterImages

Bypasses Discord's 8MB webp downscaling. Click any media to inspect at native uncompressed resolution, zoom up to 400%, rotate on the fly, and batch-download entire photo galleries.

ServerStats

Live member telemetry pinned directly above your server channels. Real-time online ratios, voice room activity counters, role demographics, and daily join rate analytics.

Timestamps

Precision message timing with automatic local timezone conversion. Hover over any chat entry to see exact millisecond creation timestamps, dynamic countdown timers, and relative offsets.

ChatFilter

Client-side regex firewall. Silently suppress ghost-pings, mute repetitive spam phrases, mask nuisance emojis, and block scam links before they enter your chat viewport.

GameActivity

Custom Rich Presence (RPC) builder. Spoof game titles, customize state banners, stream status icons, party counters, and elapsed playtime with zero external apps.

Follow User

Follow any user and automatically jump into every voice channel they enter — no manual tracking, no missed sessions.

3D Voice

Spatial 3D audio engine for Discord voice calls. Real-time HRTF positional sound calculations simulate 360-degree acoustic positioning and distance attenuation for an immersive spatial soundstage.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common technical and safety questions.

Is Lightcord safe to use on my Discord account?
Yes. Lightcord strips all client-side telemetry trackers. It uses the standard Discord desktop client user-agent and respects Discord API rate limits.
How do I enable 3D Voice and spatial audio?
Open Discord Settings → Lightcord → Plugins and enable 3D Voice. The client will calculate HRTF directional audio based on your speaker setup or stereo headphones.
Can I use BetterDiscord themes with Lightcord?
Yes, 100% compatible. Simply place any .theme.css into your themes folder or paste the theme URL directly into the Online Themes input in Lightcord Settings.
Does Lightcord interfere with the official Discord app?
No. Lightcord operates as a standalone executable in its own directory and will not modify or conflict with your official Discord client.
